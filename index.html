How the ranking works: 1) win %; 2) adjusted point differential per game; 3) head-to-head; 4) the lower-rated team gets the edge. A game counts once both scores are in and they differ.

Handicap: each game, the lower-rated team gains 1 point per 20 rating points between the two teams, and the higher-rated team loses the same. Seeds lock once all 9 round-robin games are scored.
